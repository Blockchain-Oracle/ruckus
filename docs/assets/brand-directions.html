Going with A · Arcade Cabinet

It is the only direction that is native to the flagship game (pixel Chickenz) while still framing 3D pool and the cartoon soccer as "cabinets" in one arcade. The dark ground lets the live game behind the menu and the gold payouts carry the screen, and the thick press lip gives every tap the tactile Supercell feel. From B it borrows the friendly copy ("Play with friends"); from C it borrows the ticker for live results and tournaments.
