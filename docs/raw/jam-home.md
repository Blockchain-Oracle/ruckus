08 · faq

## Questions, answered

Who provides the randomness?+

Chain's decentralized VRF. You never generate outcomes yourself; your contract consumes a verified random word. The local simulator runs a real VRF node, so what you feel locally is what players get in production.

Can I use AI?+

Yes. For code, for assets, for whatever. We use it too. But AI slop dies on the visual and sound criterion. Your game has to feel like a proper game.

What does 25% lifetime revenue mean?+

If we integrate your game into [chain.wtf](https://chain.wtf/), you earn 25% of the revenue it generates, for as long as it runs. The exact definition lives in the Terms & Conditions. Revenue is generally defined as "Bets - wins".

What if my game doesn't win?+

It can still be integrated. Integration is open to every submission, not just the top three. That's the whole point of this thing.

Why do you need my source code?+

We verify your declared RTP against your actual paytable, check SDK compliance, and screen for anything malicious. It stays private with the review team. No source, no eligibility. Trust is nice. Reading the code is better.

How do people play my game during the jam?+

You host it yourself on your own domain, and the gallery links straight to it. The local simulator is your dev environment.

How does the gallery show my game?+

Each cartridge shows a still of your game: your og:image if you set one, otherwise a screenshot we take after approval. On desktop, hovering the card plays the live game inside the cartridge if your host allows iframes. Clicking opens your hosted URL in a new tab. Add a 1200×630 og:image if you want the still to look like your game rather than a loading screen.
